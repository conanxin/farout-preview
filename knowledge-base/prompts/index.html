主题 1｜Scenes from Other Scenes, 1969（约翰·威尔科克的地下报纸网络）

原始主题: 地下出版 / 地下报刊，1969，纽约，受 Fluxus 与邮件艺术影响

P2 分数: 12

1.1 Fluxus 邮件艺术拼贴风

  • 风格名: Fluxus Mail-Art Zine Collage
  • 来源主题: Scenes from Other Scenes, 1969 — 受 Fluxus / mail-art 启发的 zine-like 报纸
  • 适合用途: X thread 头图、长文封面
  • 推荐画幅: 16:9（宽屏展示）

English Prompt: 1969 New York underground press front page, dense collage layout mixing Fluxus mail-art aesthetics with zine-style typography, hand-cut photocopied stamps, overlapping hand-stamped addresses, multiple columns of hand-set text, rubber stamp marks, postage stickers, photo Booth portraits integrated as halftone cutouts, black-and-white photocopier texture with selective magenta and yellow color wash, vintage newsprint paper grain, John Wilcock Village Voice influence, Andy Warhol aura visible in subject selection

中文 Prompt: 1969 年纽约地下报刊头版，密集拼贴版式，融合 Fluxus 邮件艺术美学与 zine 风格字体，手剪复印邮票，重叠手写邮寄地址，多栏手工排版文字，橡皮印章痕迹，邮寄贴纸，相亭肖像作为半色调剪影拼贴，黑白复印质感叠加局部洋红与黄色油墨，复古新闻纸纹理，John Wilcock 与《Village Voice》影响痕迹，Andy Warhol 气息体现在题材选择上

Negative Prompt: modern, digital, clean vector, minimalist, corporate, sterile, high-tech, smartphone, LED screens, contemporary fashion, modern architecture, 3D render, anime, stock photo, plastic texture

  • 风格名: The International Newspaper Spread

  • 适合用途: 长文配图、展览卡片
